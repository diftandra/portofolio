# Upwork Portfolio — Software Engineer

A clean, responsive one-page portfolio to link from your Upwork profile. It showcases three
service areas: **Data Entry**, **App/Web Testing (QA)**, and **Coding & Database** projects.

## Files

| File | What it is |
|------|------------|
| `index.html` | All the content and page structure |
| `styles.css` | Styling (dark modern theme, fully responsive) |
| `script.js`  | Mobile menu toggle + footer year |

## How to view it

Just open `index.html` in any web browser — no build step, no dependencies.

## What to customize (search & replace)

Open `index.html` and update these placeholders:

1. **Your Name** → your real name (appears in several places, including the logo `<YourName />`)
2. **Your City, Country** / **English, Your Language** → your location & languages
3. **Contact links** (bottom of the page):
   - `https://www.upwork.com/freelancers/~YOUR_PROFILE_ID` → your Upwork profile URL
   - `your.email@example.com` → your email
   - `https://github.com/yourusername` → your GitHub
   - `https://linkedin.com/in/yourusername` → your LinkedIn
4. **Projects section** → replace the 4 sample projects with your real work
   (add real titles, descriptions, results, and ideally screenshots/links).
5. **Stats in the hero** (`3+ years`, etc.) → your real numbers.
6. **Skills tags** → add/remove to match your actual stack.

## How to publish it (free)

Pick any one — then paste the live link into your Upwork profile:

- **GitHub Pages:** push these files to a repo, enable Pages in Settings → Pages.
- **Netlify:** drag-and-drop this folder at app.netlify.com/drop.
- **Vercel:** import the repo at vercel.com.

## Tip for Upwork

Upwork lets you add portfolio items and an external link. Use the live URL of this page as your
"personal website," and screenshot individual projects as separate portfolio items.
